About SEAIT — A Tuition-Free Institution

South East Asian Institute of Technology, Inc. (SEAIT) is a private higher education institution based in Tupi, South Cotabato, Philippines, renowned for its commitment to providing tuition-free education for all college degree programs. Established in February 2006 by Hon. Reynaldo S. Tamayo Jr. and Mrs. Rochelle P. Tamayo, the institution was founded on the belief that education should be accessible to all, regardless of financial background.

SEAIT's commitment extends beyond affordability — it is equally focused on delivering high-quality instruction and producing globally competent, values-driven graduates prepared to face the demands of an ever-evolving world.

From its early beginnings as a vocational school, SEAIT has developed into a full-fledged academic hub offering a wide range of programs.

Brief History

The founders, Reynaldo S. Tamayo Jr. and Rochelle P. Tamayo — both former Department of Science and Technology (DOST) scholars — established SEAIT as a way to give back to the community. What began as a vocational school offering TESDA programs has grown into a full academic institution offering degree programs across multiple colleges.

Milestone Timeline:

2006 – SEAIT founded; began offering TESDA 2-Year Diploma programs (Computer Programming NC IV, Computer Hardware Servicing NC II)
2007 – TESDA granted 2-year diploma in Hotel and Restaurant Management
2008 – CHED granted BS in Information Technology
2009 – CHED granted BS in Hotel Management and BS in Business Administration (Marketing Management)
2011 – CHED granted BSEd (English & Math), BEEd, and BS Civil Engineering
2013 – BS Criminology introduced
2014 – CHED granted BS Agriculture
2016 – DepEd granted recognition for the K-12 Program
2019 – BS Fisheries and BS Early Childhood Education added
2020 – CHED granted BSEd (Social Studies), BS Social Work, BS Agricultural Technology
2022 – CHED expansion: BS Agriculture (Animal & Crop Science), BSEd (Filipino & General Science)
2023 – Bachelor of Technology & Livelihood Education (ICT) added to CTE programs
