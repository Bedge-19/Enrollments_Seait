# Student Enrollment and Confirmation Checklist System — Development Prompt

Create a complete **Student Enrollment and Confirmation Checklist System** based on the workflow, user roles, business rules, and database requirements below.

The system is intended for a school/university enrollment process where students must personally visit the **Department, Registrar, Accounting, Clinic, and Security Office** to complete their enrollment/confirmation checklist.

The system must support **new students, regular students, irregular students, shifters, returnees, and transferees**.

---

---

# 5. Main Enrollment Rule

A student can only have an active/available **Confirmation Checklist** when the student's enrollment flow is:

* `Pending`
* `Approved`

The checklist must track the student's progress through:

1. Department
2. Registrar
3. Accounting
4. Clinic
5. Security Office

The student must manually process the required steps with each office.

The system records whether each step is:

* Pending
* In Progress
* Completed
* Rejected, when applicable

The student dashboard must clearly show which process has already been completed and which office still needs to be visited.

# 6. Student Registration Flow

## New Students

New students must go to the **Department** where they want to enroll.

The Department creates or assists in creating the student's account.

The Department enters information such as:

* Student personal information
* PSA information
* Birth information
* Address
* Contact information
* Program
* Student status
* Previous educational information, if applicable
* Required documents

The Department reviews and approves the student's information.

Once approved, the student can continue with the enrollment process.

---

# 7. Existing Students

Students who already have records in the system should not have their previous academic information recreated.

The system should retrieve their previous information.

Existing student records may include:

* Previous school year
* Previous program
* Previous curriculum
* Previous subjects
* Grades
* Passed subjects
* Failed subjects
* Completed subjects
* Previous enrollment records

The Department can update student details when necessary.

The Department must also be able to print previous grades when requested by the student.

---

# 8. Regular Student Flow

For regular students:

1. Retrieve the student's previous school year/program record.
2. Retrieve their previous grades.
3. Determine the curriculum currently applicable to the student.
4. Determine the subjects/units that should be taken for the current semester.
5. Generate the student's study load.
6. Generate the subject load.
7. Assign the student to the appropriate block.
8. Assign the corresponding schedule.

The system should prevent already completed subjects from being incorrectly added again.

---

# 9. Irregular Student Flow

For irregular students:

1. Retrieve the student's previous academic record.
2. Retrieve completed/passed subjects.
3. Retrieve failed subjects.
4. Determine the remaining subjects required by the curriculum.
5. Only display subjects that the student still needs to complete.
6. Subjects already passed must be credited.
7. Failed subjects must remain available for enrollment when applicable.
8. Generate the student's study load based on the remaining required subjects.
9. Assign available schedules.

The system must not add already completed subjects to the student's current study load unless they are explicitly required again.

---

# 10. Shifter Student Flow

For shifters:

1. Student selects the new program.
2. Student goes to the Department of the new program.
3. Department reviews the student's previous program.
4. Retrieve the student's previous grades.
5. Identify subjects that can be credited to the new program.
6. Apply equivalent subjects where applicable.
7. Minor subjects that are common between programs should be considered for crediting.
8. Determine the remaining subjects required by the new program.
9. Generate the study load based on credited and remaining subjects.
10. Continue to Registrar processing.

The system should maintain the student's previous program and new program.

---

# 11. Returnee Student Flow

For returnees:

1. Retrieve the student's previous school year/program.
2. Retrieve completed subjects.
3. Retrieve previous grades.
4. Determine the curriculum applicable to the student.
5. Identify subjects already completed.
6. Identify subjects still required.
7. Compare the student's completed subjects against the current curriculum.
8. Only show the subjects the student still needs to take.
9. Generate the study load.
10. Continue to Registrar processing.

The system must avoid assigning subjects that the student has already completed.

---

# 12. Transferee Student Flow

Transferees must submit their previous academic records to the Department.

The Department manually enters/reviews information such as:

* Previous school
* Previous program
* Previous subjects
* Grades
* Diploma
* Other academic documents
* PSA/personal information
* Transfer documents

The Department evaluates the previous subjects.

Only subjects that can be credited to the student's enrolled program curriculum should be credited.

The system must record:

* Original subject
* Original grade
* Previous institution
* Equivalent subject
* Equivalent unit
* Whether the subject was credited
* Reason if not credited

After Department approval, the student continues to Registrar processing.

The system must not automatically credit every previous subject.

# 23. Database Requirements — USE THIS EXACT DATABASE

**Do not design a new schema from scratch.** The database already exists and must be used as-is. The database name is **`enrollments`**, it is MySQL 8.4 / InnoDB, `utf8mb4`, and contains 35 tables plus one view (`vw_enrollment_checklist`). All primary keys, foreign keys, ENUM status values, and generated columns (e.g. `clinic.BMI`) shown below are authoritative — build every page, query, and form against these exact table and column names.

Key tables and how they map to the workflow described above:

* `users`, `login`, `staff`, `student`, `student_profile`, `student_type` — accounts, roles, and student master data
* `department`, `program`, `curriculum`, `curriculum_subject`, `subject`, `instructor`, `section`, `schedule`, `blocking` — academic structure, curriculum, and scheduling
* `academic_year`, `enrollment`, `enrollment_subject`, `evaluation`, `evaluation_subject`, `student_grades`, `credited_subject` — enrollment, subject load, and grade/credit handling for regular/irregular/shifter/returnee/transferee flows
* `admission` — Department approval step
* `payment`, `payment_item` — Accounting step
* `clinic`, `clearance` — Clinic step (note: `clinic.BMI` is a generated/stored column computed from `HeightCM` and `WeightKG` — do not write to it directly)
* `security_verification` — Security Office final verification step
* `id_validation`, `register` — ID issuance/registration support tables
* `system_log` — audit logging
* `vw_enrollment_checklist` — a ready-made view that joins `enrollment`, `student`, `admission`, `evaluation`, `payment`, `clinic`, and `security_verification` into a single per-student checklist row (`DepartmentStatus`, `RegistrarStatus`, `AccountingStatus`, `ClinicStatus`, `SecurityStatus`, `OverallStatus`). Use this view to power the Student, Security, and Admin checklist displays instead of re-deriving the status logic in PHP wherever possible.

# 32. Checklist Logic

The checklist is already database-driven via the `vw_enrollment_checklist` view, which derives status from `admission`, `evaluation`, `payment`, `clinic`, and `security_verification` for each `enrollment` row.

Conceptually the checklist maps to:

```text
EnrollmentID
StudentID
DepartmentStatus   (from admission.Status)
RegistrarStatus    (from evaluation.Status)
AccountingStatus   (from payment.PaymentStatus)
ClinicStatus       (from clinic.ClearanceStatus)
SecurityStatus     (from security_verification.Status)
OverallStatus       (CONFIRMED / IN PROGRESS)
```

Use the view directly for read paths (Student, Security, Admin dashboards). Writes go through the underlying office-specific tables (`admission`, `evaluation`, `payment`, `clinic`, `security_verification`), and the view will reflect the updated status immediately since it is a live SQL view, not a cached/duplicated table.

The student must be able to see the latest status immediately after staff actions.

# 33. Status Logic

Use the exact ENUM values already defined in the schema. Do not invent new status strings — map all UI logic to these:

* `admission.Status`: `Pending`, `Approved`, `Rejected`
* `evaluation.Status` (Registrar): defined in `evaluation` table — treat `Approved` as Registrar-completed for the checklist view
* `payment.PaymentStatus` (Accounting): `Paid` marks Accounting completed in the checklist view
* `clinic.ClearanceStatus`: `Pending`, `Cleared`, `Not Cleared` (`Cleared` marks Clinic completed)
* `security_verification.Status`: `Verified` marks Security completed and the overall enrollment `CONFIRMED`
* `enrollment.Status`: `Pending`, `Enrolled`, `Cancelled`, `Dropped`, `Irregular`, `Graduated`
* `credited_subject.Status`: `Pending`, `Approved`, `Rejected`

Do not allow invalid status transitions.

For example:

```text
Department Pending
      ↓
Department Approved
      ↓
Registrar Processing
      ↓
Registrar Completed
      ↓
Accounting Payment
      ↓
Accounting Completed
      ↓
Clinic Completed
      ↓
Security Verification
      ↓
Enrollment Confirmed
```

# 34. Validation Rules

Implement server-side and client-side validation.

Examples:

* Required student information cannot be empty.
* Student IDs (`student.StudentNo`) must be unique.
* Usernames/emails must be unique where applicable (`users` table).
* Subject codes must be unique (`subject` table).
* A student cannot enroll in the same subject twice (`enrollment_subject`).
* A student cannot be assigned conflicting schedules (`schedule` vs `enrollment_subject`).
* Already completed subjects should not be enrolled again (check `student_grades` / `credited_subject` before insert into `enrollment_subject`).
* Accounting cannot confirm an unpaid transaction (`payment.PaymentStatus`).
* Security cannot confirm an incomplete enrollment (must check `admission`, `evaluation`, `payment`, `clinic` statuses before allowing `security_verification` insert/update).
* Students cannot access another student's records.
* Staff cannot access functions outside their role.

Never rely only on JavaScript validation. PHP must also validate submitted data.

# 41. Important Business Rule

The system must preserve the difference between:

**Academic Processing** and **Checklist Confirmation**.

Do not simply mark the student as enrolled because a study load exists.

The student is only fully confirmed after the required offices complete their processes — i.e., only when `security_verification.Status = 'Verified'`, which is what drives `OverallStatus = 'CONFIRMED'` in `vw_enrollment_checklist`.

The final enrollment state must depend on the actual checklist records.
